## Draft brief 2 — What Cyprus hiring teams want from a product-design portfolio

**Status: interview draft; not ready to publish.**

The useful question is what a hiring team needs to understand from a portfolio: the problem, the designer's contribution, the decisions, and the outcome. This article will compare first-hand expectations from hiring teams rather than claiming one universal Cyprus standard.

### Interview plan

Interview [target sample] hiring designers across different company sizes and products. Request permission for each named quotation and example. Record interview dates and distinguish personal preferences from formal hiring criteria.

Questions:
1. What must a candidate explain in the first case study?
2. How do expectations differ for junior, mid-level and senior roles?
3. How should candidates present research constraints and shared team work?
4. Which evidence of outcome is persuasive when commercial metrics are confidential?
5. What makes a case study difficult to assess?
6. What should candidates know about the interview or exercise before applying?

### Article structure

- What the interviewed teams agree on: [verified findings with quotations].
- Where expectations differ: [specific examples].
- One annotated, permission-cleared case study.
- A submission checklist matched to the role.
- Links to active UI/UX and product-design jobs; make no promise that any interviewed employer is currently hiring unless verified.

No employer endorsements, hiring statistics or quotations should be invented.
